Method & sources

Six factors share a model from a German prospective cohort of 22,469 adults. The researchers estimated life expectancy at age 40 by sex, adjusted for the other measured factors, and showed that the years lost could be approximately added. This page compares the selected study categories with a reference scenario. Reference scenarios are examples, not national averages.

One millimort is a one-in-a-thousand probability of death over a specified period. The 10-year risks here are illustrative model estimates reconstructed from published hazard ratios and life expectancies. They are not measured risks for an individual. A microlife is 30 minutes of expected lifetime; the daily equivalent spreads the difference in years over the comparison group's estimated remaining life.

Associations do not establish causation. Alcohol categories describe the study rather than recommendations; do not start drinking to improve a score. BMI, diet and activity categories are broad and do not capture every aspect of health.

Method & sources

Six factors come from EPIC-Heidelberg, a German cohort of 22,469 adults followed from age 40. The authors estimated sex-specific life expectancy for each factor adjusted for the others, and found the year differences are roughly additive. Presets are example combinations, not population averages.

A millimort is a 1-in-1,000 chance of death; the 10-year figures here are reconstructed from the paper's hazard ratios and life expectancies. A microlife is 30 minutes of expected life, spread across the comparison group's remaining years.

These are observational cohort associations, not causal predictions or medical advice. In particular, the women's 0.6–1 drink/day estimate had a wide confidence interval that crossed zero, and low BMI (<22.5) often reflects existing illness.

Li, Hüsing & Kaaks (2014), EPIC-Heidelberg · Canadian sleep study · Spiegelhalter (2012), microlives

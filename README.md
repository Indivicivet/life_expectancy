# Life expectancy calculator

Compare lifestyle-associated [mortality risks and life expectancy differences](https://indivicivet.github.io/life_expectancy/).

Compare long-term habits in smoking, physical activity, body weight, alcohol, sleep duration, fruit and vegetable intake, and red and processed meat consumption. Enter your current age (18–100) and alcohol intake in UK units/week, choose a reference lifestyle and display life years, microlives per day, or estimated risk over the next 10 years in millimorts. The mortality curve is anchored to the German EPIC-Heidelberg cohort at age 40 and extrapolated to other ages. The alcohol model evaluates a published continuous dose-response fit; newer categorical results are shown for comparison. The combined model is illustrative, not clinically validated.

Layout and options fidelity etc loosely based on [climate_impact](https://github.com/indivicivet/climate_impact)

See [LIFESTYLE_RESEARCH.md](LIFESTYLE_RESEARCH.md): studies, effect sizes, confidence intervals and limitations.
See [MAGIC_NUMBERS.md](MAGIC_NUMBERS.md): model equations, assumptions and worked examples.

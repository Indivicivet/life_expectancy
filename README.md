# Life expectancy calculator

Compare lifestyle-associated [mortality risks and life expectancy differences](https://indivicivet.github.io/life_expectancy/).

Compare long-term habits in smoking, physical activity, body weight, alcohol, sleep duration, fruit and vegetable intake, and red and processed meat consumption. The model is anchored to the German EPIC-Heidelberg cohort at age 40, with activity, alcohol and sleep estimates from later mortality meta-analyses. Choose a reference lifestyle and display life years, microlives per day, or estimated 10-year millimorts. The combined model is illustrative, not clinically validated.

Layout and options fidelity etc loosely based on [climate_impact](https://github.com/indivicivet/climate_impact)

See [LIFESTYLE_RESEARCH.md](LIFESTYLE_RESEARCH.md): studies, effect sizes, confidence intervals and limitations.
See [MAGIC_NUMBERS.md](MAGIC_NUMBERS.md): model equations, assumptions and worked examples.
